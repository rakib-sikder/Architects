Glass Atrium Offices

Overview

A workplace with a garden at its heart

This mid-rise office is planned around a full-height planted atrium that carries daylight and greenery to the centre of every floorplate. Bridges and stairs cross the void, turning circulation into an event and giving staff a green outlook wherever they sit.

Operable vents at the crown drive a stack effect that ventilates the atrium naturally for much of the year, cutting energy use and softening the acoustics.
