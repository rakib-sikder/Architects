Terraced Hillside Villas

Overview

Homes that follow the land

Rather than flatten the site, we stepped six villas down the slope so each one sits on its own terrace with an uninterrupted valley view and no neighbour in its sightline. Green roofs stitch the houses back into the hillside, and shared landscaped paths weave between them.

Local stone bases anchor each villa to the ground; lightweight timber-and-glass upper levels open to the view and the breeze.
