Our story

Design that starts with a question

Founded in 2013, G3 Architects grew from a two-person studio into a full-service practice. We've always worked the same way: understand how people live and move, question the brief, then design something that will still feel right in twenty years.

From private homes to civic landmarks, we bring architectural rigour and a builder's pragmatism to every project — and we stay on site until it's done.

Gulraiz Ahmed

Principal Architect & Founder
