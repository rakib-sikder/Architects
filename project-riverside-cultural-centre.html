Riverside Cultural Centre

Overview

A civic room that opens to the river

A library and cultural centre on the Buriganga waterfront, organised around a great public stair that doubles as seating for performances. Naturally ventilated galleries wrap the reading rooms, and a rooftop terrace lifts visitors above the bustle to meet the river.

Deep brise-soleil shade the west face, while the structure is left honest and exposed — a civic building that wears its making with pride.
